What the app does, and its limits

Krasis matches the words you type against a bundled table of foods and tells you whether the result carries protein, fibre and healthy fat. It is approximate on purpose — it deals in whether a compound is present, never in grams or calories, and its verdicts are deliberately vague (“about an hour”). It will sometimes fail to recognise a food, and it will sometimes be wrong about one. It is offered as it is, without a warranty that it will be accurate or uninterrupted, to the extent the law allows.
